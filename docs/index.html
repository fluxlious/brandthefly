FAQ

    
Questions

    

      
Is this a real fly?
Real: the brain wiring (the FlyWire connectome, all 138,639 neurons), the neuron model from Shiu et al. 2024, and every neuron's position. Which neurons join in and what the brain tells the body comes from the wiring, not a script.
Not real: no animal sees your logo; it's a computer simulation. The fly's body is animated from its output neurons, and the mood labels are playful names for real neuron activity.

      
Can I choose what the fly does?
No, and that's the fun part. Your logo lights up a different set of neurons than anyone else's, so the wiring gives every brand its own reaction. Some flies turn, some reach for food, some try to escape.

      
What do I get?
A square clip of your logo firing through the brain with the fly reacting next to it, plus its dopamine verdict. Post it wherever you like.

      
Can my brand make the fly hungry?
Maybe. Appetite measures the fly's proboscis motor neuron (MN9, the one that sticks its tongue out) against a real sugar taste in the same model. Results differ by brand and a little from run to run. Its dopamine reward neurons are a tougher crowd: they have stayed quiet for every brand so far.

      
Which logos are allowed?
Your own brand or product. No hate, adult content, scams, or logos you don't have the right to use.

      
Can I buy a spot in the fly's mind?
Not yet. This is an experiment. If you'd want your brand in there, DM @fulutmd and say so.
