What's real here
  • Wiring: the FlyWire connectome (v783). Connections with 3+ synapses are kept (4.9M of 15.1M); this pruned network gives the same outputs as the full one for these stimuli.
  • Neurons: the leaky integrate-and-fire model from Shiu et al. 2024, same parameters. Targeted neurons get 150 Hz input from 20 to 320 ms.
  • LED ticker: a 5×7 dot-matrix text slides across the brain. Neurons under a lit dot get 300 Hz input while the dot passes over them.
  • Picture: every dot sits at its neuron's real position, seen from the front.
  • Fly: an animation driven by output neurons, not a physics body. Steering neurons (DNa01, DNa02) set walking and turning, MDN walks it backward, giant fiber spikes launch a takeoff, and proboscis and pharynx motor neurons drive feeding.
  • The logo shape comes from you. What the wiring does with it comes from the brain.
